What is fpgas.online?

fpgas.online lets anyone — including you! — remotely access and control a real FPGA development board over the Internet. Pick a free board, upload your bitstream or run one of the demos, and watch the result on a live camera pointed at the board's LEDs. No hardware to buy, nothing to install, and it costs nothing to use.

  • Real hardware Actual FPGA dev boards, not a simulator. Each board sits on its own Raspberry Pi that programs it and streams video.
  • Two sites, two continents Boards at the Pumping Station: One hackerspace in Chicago (USA) and in Welland (South Australia), so there is usually one free and close to you.
  • Fully open source The web app, the infrastructure-as-code, the Pi netboot images and the test designs are all on GitHub under Apache 2.0.
  • Coming soon Soon! More FPGA types, and Tiny Tapeout demo boards so you can poke at real open-source silicon too.
